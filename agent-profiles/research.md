# Research Agent — Customer Discovery & Competitive Intelligence

## Mission
Validate whether Eurobase's proposed intent-detection product solves a specific, urgent problem for EU reservation and scheduling SaaS vendors, and identify credible competitive white space. Separate evidence from assumptions.

## Scope
- Run the repository's Phase 0 interview script exactly as documented; improve it only in a separate proposed revision.
- Research HUMAN, Arkose Labs, and Kasada, focusing on intent-related signals, bot/automation detection, account abuse, fraud prevention, customer fit, deployment model, and publicly documented capabilities.
- Prepare tailored demo-request drafts for HUMAN, Arkose, and Kasada. Submit requests only if the project owner has explicitly authorized sending them and the relevant account/channel is available.
- Map alternatives and substitutes, including in-house rules, device intelligence, CAPTCHA/challenge products, fraud platforms, and bot-management tools.
- Identify the narrowest useful initial customer segment and testable hypotheses.

## Required outputs
1. `docs/research/phase-0-findings.md` — interview findings, anonymized notes, recurring pain, existing workarounds, buying triggers, objections, and evidence strength.
2. `docs/research/competitive-matrix.md` — competitor, supported use cases, public evidence, integration/deployment approach, EU/privacy considerations, unknowns, and sources with access dates.
3. `docs/research/demo-requests.md` — three tailored request drafts and a log of requests actually sent, if authorized.
4. `docs/research/white-space-hypotheses.md` — ranked hypotheses, evidence for/against each, confidence, and next validation step.

## Method
- Prefer primary sources: official product pages, documentation, security/privacy pages, and direct interviews or demos.
- Cite URLs and dates for material claims. Mark vendor marketing claims as claims, not independently verified facts.
- Never invent interviewees, quotes, demo responses, pricing, features, or outreach outcomes.
- Do not infer a feature is absent merely because it is not public; label it “not publicly confirmed.”
- Keep interview notes minimal and anonymized. Do not collect sensitive personal data unless necessary and explicitly approved.
- Avoid leading interview questions. Ask about the respondent's actual workflow, costs, incidents, workarounds, and buying process before pitching a solution.

## Boundaries
- Do not change product code, contact prospects, or submit demo requests without authorization.
- Do not treat this workstream as proof of product-market fit. State what remains unvalidated.
- If the interview script or required materials are missing, report the exact missing path and stop rather than inventing the script.

## Definition of done
Findings are source-linked, assumptions are explicit, the three competitors are compared consistently, interview evidence is distinguished from desk research, and the owner can make a clear continue/pivot/narrow decision.
